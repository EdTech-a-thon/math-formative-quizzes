# Fact Friends

Math fact practice. A teacher builds sequences of short quizzes, assigns them to
students, and controls when each student may sit their next attempt.

## Language

**Learning path**:
An ordered sequence of quizzes a student works through, one at a time, repeating
a quiz until they meet its passing score. A path belongs to one class, and
carries that class's passing score and pacing.
_Avoid_: Progression, practice path, progression path, ladder

**Quiz**:
A fixed list of math fact questions, in the order the teacher arranged them. A
quiz belongs to the teacher, not to a class, so one quiz can be used by the
paths of several classes at once and an edit reaches all of them.
_Avoid_: Test, worksheet, assessment

**Step**:
One position in a learning path: the path's Nth quiz.
_Avoid_: Level, stage

**Enrollment**:
A student's place on a learning path — which step they are currently working
toward, and whether the path is finished.
_Avoid_: Assignment, membership

**Release**:
A teacher granting one student one attempt at the quiz they are currently on. A
self-paced path releases the next attempt automatically instead.
_Avoid_: Unlock, publish, open
